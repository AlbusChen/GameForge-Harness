# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Welcome people with different levels of game-engine,
testing, and machine-learning experience. Critique claims and implementations rather than people.
Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or submit credentials and private
project data as examples.

## Project spaces

These standards apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
where someone is representing this project. Benchmark discussion must distinguish deterministic
controls, simulated-model runs, and real-model results.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, off-topic, or privacy-violating content and may limit
participation after repeated or severe violations. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through their GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
